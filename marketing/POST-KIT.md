## Posting tips

- **YouTube:** upload the 16:9 file as a regular video; also trim any 3 favorite scenes (0:18–0:33 works well) into a Short from the 9:16 file.
- **Reels:** the bottom ~25% of the 9:16 video is intentionally clear of content — Instagram's UI (caption/actions) sits there.
- **Thumbnail:** `docs/screenshots/landing.png` or the dashboard scene at 0:15 make strong thumbnails.
- Post the Reel and the YouTube video at different times; cross-link in comments.
